# Content Guidelines

Posts must be relevant to cybersecurity operations, threat intelligence, or opensecstack platform usage.

## Encouraged
- Incident post-mortems (anonymised)
- Detection recipe write-ups (SIGMA, Elastic, Splunk)
- NIS2 compliance guides
- CSAF advisory walkthroughs
- SecureLab scenario results

## Not allowed
- Active exploit code without responsible disclosure
- Personal attacks or harassment
- Off-topic content

Violations are handled via the moderation queue (moderator+ role).
